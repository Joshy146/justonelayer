Application & Care Guide

Step-by-step instructions to ensure your decals and goods last as long as possible.

How to Apply Car Decals

  1. Clean the surface thoroughly with rubbing alcohol and let it dry completely.
  2. Firmly rub a squeegee or credit card across the decal over the clear transfer tape to ensure the vinyl adheres to the tape.
  3. Slowly peel the paper backing away at a sharp angle. If the vinyl sticks to the paper, press it back down and rub again.
  4. Position the decal on the surface, press down firmly, and slowly peel away the clear transfer tape.

Apparel Care Instructions

  • Turn hoodies, sweatpants, and shirts inside out before washing.
  • Machine wash cold with like colors on a gentle cycle.
  • Tumble dry on low heat or hang to air dry for maximum longevity.
  • Do not dry clean or iron directly over the vinyl design.
