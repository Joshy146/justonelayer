Help & Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your shipping policies and rates?

All orders over $30 qualify for FREE shipping! For orders under $30, customers located within 50 miles of Walker, Louisiana receive local delivery/pickup options, while orders going 50+ miles out incur a standard shipping fee.

Have you tested your products?

Yes! We thoroughly test each product ourselves and have actively used them in real-world conditions for over a year before offering them to our customers.

Are your decals weather-resistant? Will they fade?

Our decals are weather-resistant and built to handle rain, wind, and outdoor exposure. However, like any vinyl exposed to harsh, direct sunlight day after day over an extended period, the colors will eventually experience natural fading.

What are the size limits for custom decals?

We make custom vinyl decals up to a maximum size of 2 feet long by 1 foot wide. Pricing scales based on the size tier you choose.

What are the print size limits for apparel (shirts and pants)?

All custom graphics and prints on shirts, pants, and our Gym Rat packages are restricted to a maximum size of 8.5 x 11 inches.

Are coffee mugs available?

Classic coffee mugs are currently sold out, but we offer insulated stainless steel tumblers and glass can tumblers!
