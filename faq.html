Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our products, decals, and shipping.

How durable are the car decals?

Our outdoor-grade single-layer vinyl is weather-resistant, UV-resistant, and built to withstand car washes and highway elements for years.

How do I wash apparel with custom decals?

Wash inside out in cold water on a gentle cycle. Tumble dry on low heat or hang dry. Do not iron directly on the decal.

What makes donkey milk soap special?

Donkey milk is packed with vitamins and natural fatty acids that nourish, moisturize, and soothe sensitive skin unlike standard commercial soap bars.
